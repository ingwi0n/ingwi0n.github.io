    Returns: Alaska Division of Elections precinct results, unofficial. This page is a saved copy and does not update on its own. Alaska elects by ranked choice, and these are first choices. The state does not call races, so hatching marks a leader under 50%, whose race ranked choices will decide once counting ends. Independents are candidates registered nonpartisan or undeclared, and legislators elected that way. Flips compare the leader with the party holding the seat in the 2025–26 Legislature, including this term's appointments. Senate seats not on the ballot count for the party that holds them. Both chambers have been run by bipartisan coalitions, so party totals are not the same as control. U.S. Senate, U.S. House and governor: first choices by House district from the same precinct file; Senate districts add their two House districts, and overseas federal ballots count only statewide. The “vs.” views take each legislative race that has a Republican and at least one Democrat or independent, and compare those candidates' combined share of first choices with the statewide candidate's share in the same district. District lines: Census 2026 TIGER/Line files clipped to the 1:500,000 shoreline. Each Senate district joins two House districts. 2024 president: two-party margin by current district, from VEST precinct results via Dave's Redistricting, allocated to districts by census block. The tick on the scale marks the statewide result.
